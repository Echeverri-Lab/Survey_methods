# Scale Decisions and Population Equivalence

*Notes on selection of psychometric scales, adaptations made, and considerations for equivalence across different populations.*

## 2026 revision — literature curation pass

Reviewed against the 45 candidate papers (Step 3 topic match) from the
25% random subsample described in
`Relevant Literature/search_2026/exports/suggested_new_citations.md`.

- **Added** to `scales_reference_table.md`: SINS (state, as opposed to trait,
  nature connection — Hanley et al., 2022), a Polish 7-item Connectedness to
  Nature Scale adaptation (Bajkowska et al., 2026), the Environmental
  Value-Bases Scale (5-culture validated instrumental/intrinsic/relational
  values measure — Lengieza et al., 2025), and two eco-anxiety/climate-distress
  instruments not previously represented in the table: the Hogg Eco-Anxiety
  Scale (Italian adaptation, Rocchi et al., 2023) and the Climate Anxiety
  Scale (Polish adaptation, 2022). These meet C2 (fill a construct gap —
  eco-anxiety/climate distress had no prior entry) and C3 (cross-cultural
  adaptation as a worked example).
- **Not added:** most of the remaining candidates were psychometric
  validations of scales unrelated to socio-ecological constructs (e.g., job
  crafting, gender climate in schools, virtual-meeting interruptions) or
  additional TPB-based studies already covered under Step 2's curation
  decision. These remain in the master corpus for reference.
